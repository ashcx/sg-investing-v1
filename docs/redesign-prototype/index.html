    Data & methods

    A short guide to the evidence and assumptions a real result should show.

    Coverage

    Know which dates are usable

    This wireframe has no connected market data. A real result should distinguish requested dates from the actual security and FX dates used, and explain incomplete or unavailable coverage.

    Security listings

    The small catalog here is illustrative. Ticker, exchange, currency, distribution policy, and price history would come from the published catalog and data packs.

    Sources and provenance

    A real result should identify its data snapshot, supported period, methodology version, and warnings that affect interpretation.

    Calculation approach

    Observed data plus clear assumptions

    Prices and dates

    Prices are daily and unadjusted. A purchase uses the next available trading day; valuation uses the previous available trading day.

    Dividends and tax

    Dividends are modeled separately from prices. Applicable withholding rules depend on security, country, and effective date; missing rules should be disclosed.

    Currency

    FX is expressed as SGD per unit of foreign currency. A real comparison should identify native and SGD values clearly.

    Costs and portfolio limits

    The current engine does not model brokerage, sale, FX-conversion, or slippage costs, and does not calculate Singapore capital-gains tax. Portfolio reporting uses weighted-average cost basis.
