How calculations work

Trading dates

Daily local-exchange closes are canonical. Purchase dates resolve forward to the next trading day; valuation dates resolve back to the previous one.

Currency conversion

FX rates show SGD per unit of foreign currency.

Dividends and tax

Set dividends, withholding tax and reinvestment independently. Accumulating funds pay no investor cash dividends.

Data warnings

Estimated pay dates, missing tax rules and incomplete history remain attached to the result and its export.
