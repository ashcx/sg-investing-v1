What are WAC and mark-to-market?

WAC is weighted-average cost, a reporting convention for the cost of open holdings. Mark-to-market values those holdings at the selected as-of price.

Nothing material is hidden.

Every result carries the assumptions, data-quality status and methodology version used for that replay.

01

Price first.

Daily local-exchange closes are canonical. Purchase dates resolve forward to the next trading day; valuation dates resolve back to the previous one.

02

FX stays explicit.

One unit of native currency equals X SGD. That lets you separate what the asset did from what the currency did.

03

Dividends are a choice.

Toggle dividends, investor-level withholding tax and reinvestment independently. Accumulating funds never receive invented cash.

04

Warnings travel.

Estimated pay dates, missing tax rules and incomplete history remain attached to the result and its export.
